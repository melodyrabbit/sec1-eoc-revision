Food Safety & Kitchen Hazards

Keep your food safe and avoid lab accidents.

Temperature Rules

  • 5°C to 60°C Temperature Danger Zone: Bacteria multiply rapidly.
  • < 5°C Storage: Keep raw meat on bottom shelf of chiller so juices don't drip.
  • 74°C Cooking: Internal temp required for poultry.
  • 75°C Reheating: Leftovers must reach this temp for 2 mins.

Contamination Types

Physical

Hair, glass, staples in food.

Chemical

Bleach, insecticides mixing with food.

Microbial

Bacteria (grows at 37°C), Yeasts (spoilage), Moulds (mycotoxins).

Lab Safety Audit

Hover/Click on the hazards to reveal safe practices:

Fires/Scalds

Ensure utensils are dry before frying to prevent oil splatter.

Cuts

NEVER place knives into the wash basin. Wash them separately.

Slips

Wipe up spills immediately. Close all cabinet doors.

Unattended

Always use a timer. Keep paper towels away from open flames.

Smart Money Management

Master your budget and spend wisely.

Key Concepts (Click to reveal)

Need vs. Want

Need: Necessary for survival (e.g., clean water, basic clothing).

Want: Desirable but not essential (e.g., latest phone, branded shoes).

50/30/20 Rule

A budgeting guide to divide your income:

  • 50% Needs (Essentials)
  • 30% Wants (Extras)
  • 20% Savings (Future)

4 Decision Questions

Before buying, ask:

  1. Do I already have the item?
  2. Can I find something cheaper?
  3. Am I buying it because it's branded?
  4. Will I be able to live without it?

Payment Modes

Cash: Widely accepted, easy to budget, but can be lost.

Debit Card: Deducts directly from bank account.

Credit Card: Buy now, pay later. Risk of high interest & debt.

Knowledge Check

The Discerning Consumer

Know your rights and shop smart.

A Discerning Consumer shows good judgement when making a purchase. They research, budget, and avoid impulse buying.

In-Store Shopping

  • Pro: Try on items, see actual product.
  • Pro: Immediate takeaway.
  • Con: Crowds, long queues.
  • Con: Restricted by opening hours.

Online Shopping

  • Pro: Large variety, shop 24/7.
  • Pro: Easy price comparison.
  • Con: Cannot try before buying.
  • Con: Delivery wait times & shipping fees.

Consumer Rights & Protection

The Lemon Law

Protects consumers against defective goods. You can request repair, replacement, price reduction, or refund.

EXCEPTIONS (When it does NOT apply):
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Consumer misused the item.
  • Consumer tried to repair it themselves.
  • Change of mind (you simply don't like it anymore).
